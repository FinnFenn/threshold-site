§ Privacy

Privacy.

Last updated 19 November 2026.

Threshold Formations Ltd runs this site.

What we hold. If you join the waitlist, your email address. If you apply, your name, email and answers.

Why. To tell you when applications open, for Expedition I and future expeditions. To read your application and reply to it. Nothing else.

Who sees it. The founders. It is kept with the providers that host this site and store our records. We do not sell it or pass it on.

How long. The waitlist: until you ask us to remove you. Applications: twelve months, then deleted.

Your rights. You can ask to see, correct or delete what we hold. Write to info@thresholdresidency.org. If you are unhappy with how we handle it, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Analytics. We count visits without cookies and without following you across other sites.
